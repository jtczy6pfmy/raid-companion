Connect your shared account

Paste a HellHades public read-only sharing URL. This feature stores the URL only on this iPad browser; it does not request RAID credentials or read HellHades account data.

Anyone holding your share URL may view the shared account. Use a short expiration and revoke it in HellHades when no longer needed. This URL is not a password.
